Gruzzler

Gruzzler is a unique puzzle game that challenges your logical thinking, optimization, and problem-solving skills.

Unlike traditional number puzzles, Gruzzler allows you to discover the clues yourself.

When you're done you can share your clues!

Please play, my degree is in bioinformatics.

Rules

Each number appears once per column
Each number appears once per row
Each number appears once per house

Clues

Single
Double
Target

Arrows point toward the nearest matching number. Target means you found it!

Modes

Search

Discover clues by entering numbers in cells

Scratch

Eliminate impossible numbers from cells

Solve

Place your final answers to complete the puzzle

During the pandemic, like a lot of people, I got hooked on Cracking The Cryptic. Watching Simon and Mark solve impossible-looking puzzles became a nightly ritual.

A friend challenged me: could I create a logic puzzle that was discoverable - where you find your own clues - but used no math?

Gruzzler became my nights-and-weekends project. When I wasn't in the lab solving genetics puzzles, I was working on this. It took longer than I'd like to admit.

I hope you enjoy it.

- Bridget
